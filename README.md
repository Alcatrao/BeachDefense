# BeachDefense — Codex breaking surf

Praia de Mira with live nearshore breaking water, cyan sky reflections, procedural embossed wavelets, whitewater, splash bursts and wet collars. Serve the repository over HTTP and open index.html. beach.html provides whole-beach, tube and rider inspection views. WASD steers, Space jumps, V changes the game view, B resets the surf and Escape pauses.

The narrow FLIP/PIC section evolves under gravity and pressure over the original shelf. There is no timed replay or prescribed tube animation. One extracted contour is shared along the curved beach. The single-valued gameplay height/velocity field comes from the liquid particles, independently of the render mesh. Character buoyancy, relative-flow drag and planing forces respond to incoming surf and backwash; the camera stays upright. To meet the requested lightweight CPU height model, the character no longer has a second fluid volume or sends reciprocal momentum into the water. The height envelope cannot resolve the air under an overhanging lip. The old finite-body model remains in the source history and js/coast/surf-physics.mjs.

Water and foam use original procedural textures and a capped instanced splash/ring pool. No Age of Mythology assets or owner screenshots are included. Software rendering is detected automatically; it uses half pixel ratio,20 coast columns, cheap normals, anisotropy1 and128 effect slots (hardware48 columns and640 slots). The grid concentrates detail around the camera and nearshore. Automated browsers never request pointer lock; normal manual play retains mouse capture.

This is still a surf prototype. Coherent waves are extended along215 m of beach from a narrow physical section; their alongshore uniformity and the distant analytical waves are approximations. Arc-length contour strands, bounded fairing and a curvature-aware coast grid reduce pinches, shading creases and coarse shoreline bends. Residual coarse-fluid folds and parallel alongshore banding remain. A calibrated clean2 m barrel and completely natural shorebreak are unfinished. Water rendering updates at physical snapshots, about30 Hz, although the hardware display can render at60 Hz. Foam and spray are visual tracers, not an air/bubble solve. Procedural texture scales hide repetition but do not prove every viewpoint artifact-free.
