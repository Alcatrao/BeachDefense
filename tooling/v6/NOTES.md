# v6 methods and research — Codex, 17–18 October 2026

The implementation continues the existing optimized FLIP/PIC solver rather than prescribing a curling mesh. Primary method reference: https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/tenMinutePhysics/18-flip.html . The source band uses approximate linear depth-profile orbital forcing offshore, then pressure, gravity and a reef bed evolve the nearshore liquid. It is not a full calibrated laboratory wavemaker transfer function.

Research consulted for generation and breaking: https://ascelibrary.org/doi/full/10.1061/%28ASCE%29WW.1943-5460.0000420 (controlled piston generation); https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1370255/ (Lagrangian breaking-wave/beach work); https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01925 (focused plunging breaker investigation); https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00827 (SPH surf-zone modeling); https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/energy-research/articles/10.3389/fenrg.2020.00133/full (wavemaker/absorption methods). These inform design experiments, not independent validation of this code. Sponge damping in the shoreward z<3 m zone is an approximate absorber, not a natural beach friction model.

Pressure grid and marker spacing are configurable through makeFluidSolver. A narrow symmetric section reduces background simulation work; a high-resolution physical run is recorded for play. Standard separation regularizes background marker clumping. Symmetry and separation are disabled in the body volume. The actual cache contains no authored curl or extra kick. It does have a discontinuous loop and is not a live coupled full-width fluid.

Marching squares extracts a y/z contour of the cached particle-density field, including overhangs. A short visual density filter and bed-connected mask suppress detached spray sheets. It is extruded along the shoreline with smooth gradient normals. This is a render approximation; character contact uses actual local particles, not the smoothed far mesh. Local surface nets reconstruct the live 3D volume. White foam intensity follows falling-water/impact tracers and body contact, with decay and a fixed point cap.

validate.mjs simulates the live section for sixty seconds, checks finite/count/speed, and scores connected-roof candidates. Its cavity test excludes isolated high spray, requires liquid above and below a gap and checks roof connectivity to the largest liquid component. Cavity measurements depend on a finite density grid/threshold and are not scientific tube certificates. cache-validation.mjs checks the exact bundled binary and reports the loop parcel mismatch. contact.mjs checks reciprocal linear impulse and matched wave/calm entry at the front of the wave; calm is only required upright through two seconds, not indefinitely. browser.cjs serves installed source, closes server/browser in finally, records renderer identity, screenshots, standalone and full-game timing, pause/resume and defense entry. It requires the existing local Playwright and Edge installation; no new packages were installed.
